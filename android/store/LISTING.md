## Full description (Play, 4000 characters max)

```
Toolz is a pocket workshop: 85+ everyday tools that answer instantly and keep
your numbers to yourself. Every calculation runs on your phone. Nothing you type
is uploaded, there is no account to create, and the tools work with no signal.

WHAT YOU GET

Text — count words and characters with reading time, change case, strip
duplicate lines, sort lines, join line breaks, build URL slugs, generate lorem
ipsum, reverse text, find and replace.

Calculators — percentages and percentage change, VAT both ways, BMI with the
healthy-weight range, exact age, days between dates, loan repayments with total
interest, compound interest, discounts, tip splitting, mean and median, standard
deviation, Roman numerals, random draws.

Money — savings goals with a monthly figure, margin versus markup, break-even
units, return on investment, salary to hourly and back.

Developer — Base64, URL encoding, JSON formatting and validation, UUIDs, MD5 and
SHA hashes, Unix timestamps, cryptographically random hex, number bases, colour
conversion.

Everyday — unit conversion across length, mass, volume and temperature, data
sizes, trip fuel cost, paint quantities, tile counts, a shopping total.

Security — long passwords generated with a secure random source, password
strength in bits with crack-time estimates, numeric PINs.

Study — weighted grade averages, the mark you need on a final exam, a revision
plan split across the days you have left.

SEO — title and description length checks, a phone-sized search preview, keyword
density, a robots.txt builder, campaign URL tagging.

Business — invoice totals with tax, payback periods, pricing tiers, commission
splits.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT

• Answers appear as you type. There is no button to press and no spinner.
• Each result shows its workings, so you can see where the number came from.
• Star the tools you use weekly and they move to the top of the home screen.
• Light and dark, sized for one hand, labelled for screen readers.
• Onboarding takes twenty seconds and can be replayed from Settings.

PRIVACY

No account. No analytics. No profile. Your favourites, recent tools and theme
choice live in the app's private storage on your device and can be cleared at any
time. The only network traffic is advertising, served by Google AdMob once you
have agreed to it — and you can change that choice whenever you like.

Toolz is free because of those ads. Banners sit at the foot of a tool, never
inside a result, and a rewarded ad can hide them for an hour if you prefer.

Toolz also runs in a browser at toolz.name.ng.
```
